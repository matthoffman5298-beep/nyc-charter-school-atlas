    About this map.

    This independent atlas displays charter school records from the NYS ITS Geospatial Services / NYSED school-location layer ↗, supplemented with reviewed school and NYC Public Schools campus locations. Individual additions and corrections include their supporting sources in the school details.

    Records are mapped school points, not a verified census of separate campuses. Multiple records may share a location. Map clusters count records, not buildings.

    The source may lag openings, closures, relocations, or name changes. This atlas does not verify grades, enrollment, admissions, or school quality. Confirm current details directly with each school and the NYSED charter school directory ↗.

    The school list and map controls work from bundled files. An internet connection is needed for background map tiles and external links.
